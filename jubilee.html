Calling all Gallery people

Once a Gallery musician, always part of the family.

Were you part of Gallery Music Centre or Gallery Community Orchestra over the years? We'd love to see you again.

We're inviting former and current members and associates to join in the rehearsal and performance, or to come along for the shared lunch and concert. You don't need to perform to be part of the celebration.

  • Past students
  • Parents & whānau
  • Teaching staff
  • Orchestra players
  • Conductors
  • Administrators

Know someone who was involved years ago? Please send them this page. We'd love to reconnect with as many members of the Gallery community as possible.
