Calling all Gallery people

Come back and join us

Past and present students, players, teachers, conductors, parents and volunteers are invited to join the rehearsal and performance, or catch up over a shared lunch. You’re welcome whether or not you play.

Optional Saturday rehearsals

You can also join regular rehearsals at Dyer Street School, Epuni, in the weeks leading up to the Jubilee: orchestra, Saturdays 9:30–11:30am; Pat Sviatko Ensemble with orchestra, 9:30–10:30am. Indicate your interest in the registration form.
